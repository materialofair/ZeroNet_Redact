# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People working on an iPhone or iPad who need to remove identifying information from photos, PDFs, and videos before sharing them. The primary video workflow is selecting a local video, reviewing automatically detected face covers, choosing a cover and optional voice preset, then exporting a safe copy.

## Product Purpose

ZeroNet Redact is a fully offline privacy-redaction tool. It keeps source files on the device, processes them locally, and gives users a clear, reviewable path from private original to shareable redacted output. Success means users can confidently understand what was detected, what will be changed, and when the exported file is ready without uploading anything or creating an account.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, calm, direct. The interface should reduce anxiety around accidental disclosure, explain automated processing plainly, and keep attention on the user's file and review decisions.

## Anti-references

Avoid ad-driven utility patterns, cloud-upload-first flows, noisy gamification, entertainment-style face effects, decorative gradients that compete with the preview, and dense controls that hide the privacy consequence of an action.

## Design Principles

- Show the privacy consequence before the export action: make the preview and selected protections easy to verify.
- Explain automation without overclaiming: detection is helpful, not infallible; review guidance stays visible at the right moment.
- Keep the task local and focused: progressive disclosure should prevent secondary voice controls from competing with face protection.
- Make every state recoverable: loading, cancellation, failure, no-face detection, and completion all provide a clear next step.
- Preserve platform trust: use familiar iOS controls, accessible touch targets, dynamic type, and reduced-motion-friendly transitions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA-equivalent contrast where applicable, support Dynamic Type without clipping, maintain at least 44pt touch targets, provide descriptive accessibility labels for icon-only actions, keep status updates understandable without relying on color alone, and respect Reduce Motion. The interface should remain usable in both light and dark appearances.
