# Check the exported copy: photos, videos and PDFs

A developer-maintained Cloakmark workflow guide, prepared with AI assistance from published product documentation. Updated 10 November 2026. This checklist does not certify anonymization or legal compliance.

## Start with a harmless test

Use a sample containing fictional details before relying on a new workflow. Keep your original separate, and give the exported copy a clear name so you do not accidentally send the original.

## Photos and screenshots

- Review the whole image, not just the first detected name or address.
- Add manual covers for details that detection missed. Use opaque covers for readable private text.
- Open the actual exported image and inspect it at a useful zoom level. An editing preview alone is not the final deliverable.

## Videos

Automatic face-detection assistance and manual covers behave differently. Face covers can follow detected faces; a manual region stays in a fixed position during its selected time interval. Video OCR and general object tracking are not supported.

Play the saved export from beginning to end. Watch transitions, scrolling details and the first and last frames. Listen as well: a covered face can still be accompanied by a spoken name or other identifying audio. Voice effects do not erase what was said or guarantee anonymity. Consider whether the mute option better matches the purpose of the shared copy.

If a cover misses a moving detail, revise the edit and inspect the next export again. Do not assume one successful frame proves the whole clip is covered.

## PDFs

A rectangle that merely sits above text is different from removing the underlying text. Cloakmark removes text beneath redacted areas during PDF export. Reopen that exported PDF, inspect each page, and test search, selection and copying around the covered details.

These checks do not establish that every attachment, hidden object or metadata field has been sanitized. High-consequence documents may need a more specialized review process.

## Product and pricing boundaries

Cloakmark processes content on iPhone or iPad without uploading originals to a redaction service. Apple purchase and restoration services can use the network. The free tier allows three photo/video exports combined per day and three PDF exports per day, with a 300 MB free source-video cap. The optional one-time upgrade removes those limits. Check the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6756290503) for current regional pricing.

## 中文复核清单

- 图片：检查整张图片，不能只遮住检测到的第一处信息；放大检查真正导出的图片。
- 视频：完整观看导出文件并试听声音。手动框不会跟随滚动文字；人脸检测也可能遗漏。
- PDF：重新打开导出副本，逐页查看，并检查搜索、选择和复制。视觉上盖住文字，不等于所有隐藏信息都已清理。
- 分享前确认选择的是导出副本，而不是原件。本清单不构成匿名保证、法律合规证明或独立安全审计。
